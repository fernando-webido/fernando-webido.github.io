Music Distribution Platform
UX ResearchWorkflow DesignSaaSUnosquare
Key impact −55% Steps in artist publishing
workflow (9 → 4 steps)
2022–2024 + Case study
The problem

Artists had to complete a 9-step publishing workflow to distribute their music. The process was fragmented, unclear, and full of redundant data entry — causing high drop-off before completion and frustration with the platform.

My role
  • Led end-to-end UX from discovery through handoff
  • Conducted 12 user interviews with artists & label managers
  • Synthesized pain points into actionable design principles
  • Prototyped and iterated in Figma with weekly user feedback cycles
Approach
  • Journey mapping revealed 4 redundant steps
  • Applied progressive disclosure to surface only what's needed
  • Built a smart defaults system to pre-fill repeated fields
  • Redesigned information architecture around artist mental models
Process
01 · Discover
User interviews

12 sessions with artists and label managers to map the current journey and identify drop-off points.

02 · Define
Journey mapping

Mapped all 9 steps against user goals — identified 4 steps as redundant or combinable without data loss.

03 · Design
Prototype & test

3 rounds of usability testing on prototypes. Each iteration cut task completion time by an average of 18%.

04 · Deliver
Handoff

Annotated specs delivered to engineering, with reusable components added to the existing design system.

Before → After
Before
  • 9 separate screens to complete
  • Repeated data entry across steps
  • No progress indicators
  • High abandonment mid-flow
  • Multiple error states with no guidance
→
After
  • 4 consolidated, logical steps
  • Smart defaults pre-fill known info
  • Clear progress bar throughout
  • Significant drop in task abandonment
  • Inline validation with clear guidance
